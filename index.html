Informal Taxation in Development Projects: The Role of Chiefs in Sierra Leone

Field experiment · Sierra Leone

When chiefs mobilize labor for public goods, who actually pays? Randomized selection methods measure whether citizens take costly action to avoid the call — and whether the burden lands on the poor.

PAPER
2023

Candidate Entry Into Local Government

with Katherine Casey, Niccoló Meriggi, Abou Bakarr Kamara · Randomized trial · Sierra Leone

How do you get high-integrity, representative citizens to stand for local office? We evaluate an intervention designed with government and civil-society partners ahead of Sierra Leone's 2023 local council elections.

PAPER
now

Improving the Use of Zambia's Constituency Development Fund (CDF)

with Katherine Casey, Jeremy Bowles, Shahrukh Wani · Intervention design with government partners · Zambia

Zambia massively scaled its Constituency Development Fund to decentralize public infrastructure spending. We work with government partners on how the fund's design shapes what communities actually get.

2022

Too Much or Too Little Faith in Success? Experimental Evidence from Colombian Entrepreneurs

with Réka Zempléni · Lab-in-the-field · Colombia

Most new businesses in Colombia fail. Do entrepreneurs enter because they misjudge their odds? We separate the roles of ambiguity preferences, confidence, and optimism.

STANFORD, CALIFORNIA

I’m a development economist working on governance and public goods in West Africa and Latin America. As Impact & Learning Manager at Stanford Impact Labs, I design how a funder learns from a portfolio of applied social science — my job is to make evidence usable, not just rigorous.

Before Stanford Impact Labs, my fieldwork took me to Sierra Leone, Zambia and Colombia, working with chiefs, councils and entrepreneurs on how institutions shape everyday economic life.
