Development economist · Impact & Learning, Stanford Impact Labs

I work on both sides of the research–impact pipeline. I run field experiments on governance and public goods in Sierra Leone, Zambia and Colombia; at Stanford Impact Labs I lead measurement and learning for a portfolio of applied social science. The common thread: rigorous evidence matters most when someone can actually use it.

Practice

How does a funder measure the impact of a research portfolio — before the impact exists? That question is my day job.

At Stanford Impact Labs I build the rubrics, theories of change, and portfolio-level evidence syntheses that turn a diverse set of applied research investments into institutional learning — evaluation designed to be used, not performed.
