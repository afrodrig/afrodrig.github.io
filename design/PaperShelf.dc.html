The Paper Wheel — interaction storyboard
papers orbit a circular axis; scroll rotates, focus enlarges, click unfolds. Spring physics throughout (no fixed-duration easing).
1
At rest. Papers sit on a circular axis anchored off the right edge — a wheel, not a list. The focal card is inked; neighbors recede in scale and opacity along the arc.
2
Scroll rotates the wheel. The wheel carries momentum and snaps card-by-card with a spring (slight overshoot, natural settle). Keyboard arrows and drag do the same — interruptible at any moment.
3
Click unfolds. The focal card expands in place into a visual abstract — key figure animates in, one-sentence finding, PDF/BibTeX/data. The wheel recedes but stays live at the edge; ESC folds everything back.
